Fusion methods on public crowdsourcing truth-inference datasets (workers play the part of
outlets). 5-fold cross-validation over gold items, seed 2024; each fold is scored with the
other folds' truth known. AUC 95% interval: percentile bootstrap, 2,000 resamples.
Run on 1-2 November 2026 with `python -m carcux_data.j24 fuse-bench` (commit of this file).
Dawid-Skene learns the share of true items; CARCUX uses the Beta(2,1) prior.

Reading: CARCUX's ranking advantage grows as answers get scarce (RTE, 1 answer: +0.015 over
Dawid-Skene, +0.051 over reliability) and vanishes with 5 or more. With 1 answer per item its
probabilities are less well calibrated than reliability's (ECE 0.090 vs 0.061 on RTE, 0.086
vs 0.060 on TEMP); unsupervised Dawid-Skene is badly calibrated there (0.163, 0.311).
